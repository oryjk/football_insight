# Phoenix Bowl

Phoenix Bowl treats a football stadium as a living night landscape: a luminous basin, a field of pressure, and a ring of supporters becoming part of the architecture. Space is the main material. The upper half stays calm and dark so product UI can breathe; the lower half gathers force through stacked curves, field geometry, and crowd density.

The visual language is architectural rather than photographic. The final image should suggest Chengdu Phoenix Hill Professional Football Stadium through a broad bowl silhouette, strong floodlights, tiered stands, and compressed supporter rhythm, without copying a particular photograph, logo, banner, or club identity. The reference should be felt by people who know the place, while still working as an original football background.

Color is disciplined and practical: cool night blues and graphite structure, bright white stadium light, vivid pitch green, and restrained supporter accents in warm red and gold. The marks should feel painstakingly placed, like a dense hand-built diagram rather than a quick texture. Crowd energy comes from thousands of small points and sweeping banners, not readable slogans.

The composition is mobile-first at 1080 x 1920. Stadium structure sits in the middle third, leaving atmospheric headroom above and darker foreground below for overlays, buttons, or cards. Edges remain subdued; the brightest energy stays near the stand and pitch so the image gives the app a sense of place without fighting the interface.

Craft matters more than literal detail. Geometry should be crisp where the stadium reads as built form, while light beams, haze, and foreground shadow soften the image into a usable background. The intended result is a polished, high-density original asset that feels designed for a football data product, not a generic sports poster.
